Playing with the flesh of the [[right hand]] fingers is mostly found in [[acoustic|acoustic guitar]] traditions such as [[fingerpicking]], [[boom chick]] or [[blues]]. It contrasts with [[classical right hand|classical guitar right hand]] where we use long nails to pluck the strings.

Playing with the flesh provides a softer and quieter tone and also offer a lower maintenance than playing with [[nails]]. The [[attack]] is less pronounced but produces no [[scratching]] sound. 

Playing with the flesh on [[acoustic|steel string guitar]] can be hard on the fingertips at first but you will grow calluses quite fast. 

I would advise most beginners who want to play with their fingers to start with flesh and eventually move on to playing with [[nails]] if they desire after a few months/years.
