Archtops are a type of guitar featuring a construction that resembles the one found in instruments of the string quartet, violin, viola or cello.

Instead of having a flat [[sound board]] with [[bracing]], archtop have a sculpted or molded top with a typical curve. 

They often make use of f holes similar to violins instead of the [[sound hole]]. They also don't use the same type of [[bridge]] found in acoustic, they tend to have a floating bridge held in place by the tension of the strings and a tailpiece where the strings are tied. While they can use standard [[electric guitar strings]], they often are strung with [[flat wound]] strings offering a warmer tone.

They have existed since the start of the 20th century and are mostly used in [[jazz]].
