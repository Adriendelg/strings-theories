- [ ] do your own table

Using only common open G, C and D shapes, [[tonality|keys]] that are easy to play in [[standard tuning]] making use of a lot of [[open strings]] and [[open chords]] as well as a [[capo]] between fret 1 and 5 you can play in all 12 keys even having options between one shape or another for some.

This is an easy way to navigate basic [[harmony]] on the [[fretboard]] with minimal learning.
