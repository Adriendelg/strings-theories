Polyphony refers to music where multiple [[melody|melodies]] happen at the same time. A polyphonic instrument, such as guitar or piano, can play multiple [[pitch|pitches]]
simultaneously. This is in contrast with monophonic instruments such as saxophone or flute who can only play one note at a time.

The term is sometimes employed to mean [[counterpoint]] which is a specific type of polyphonic music with particular rules.
