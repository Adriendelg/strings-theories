classical guitar can refer to three things:
- a [[types|guitar type]]
- a [[music genres|musical genre]] 
- a type of instrumental [[technique]] see [[classical guitar technique]]

# as a guitar type, the traditional classical guitar
- standard, smaller body shape
- nylon strings
- Spanish construction, Torres : 650-655mm scale length, cedar or spruce tops, rosewood/cypress back and sides
- Antonio de Torres, Hermann Hauser, José Ramírez, Daniel Friederich
- Fan bracing
- clear separation between bass and treble
- 12th fret neck joint
- wider neck (50-52mm)

# classical guitar as a [[repertoire]]
- centuries of classical music and contemporary creations
