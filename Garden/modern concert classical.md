Modern concert classical guitars are the contemporary evolution of the [[classical|classical guitar]], it features the same body shape but most of the internals and playability elements have been rethought. 

The [[bracing]] is often different, departing from the traditional fan bracing, modern innovations in the classical guitar world include the double top, a type of sandwich of wood and composite material or a [[lattice bracing]] with a beehive-like pattern.

They often also have an elevated fretboard offering better access to the high frets and sometimes a [[truss rod]] borrowed from the world of [[acoustic|acoustic guitar]].

Some notable luthiers are Greg Smallman, Matthias Dammann and Simon Marty.

These guitars are usually much louder than a traditional classical, they are made to strive with higher string tension and have an increased projection better suited to larger halls.
