The blues is great musical tradition that is an essential to understanding modern music and a great vessel for learning the guitar. It is a key element of western music and has influenced many other genres. 

Blues is a [[music genres|musical genre]], a [[form]] and a [[musical language]]. 

# blues as a genre
Blues originates from African-Americans in the south of the US around 1900. It comes from [[call and response]] songs originating among slaves and evolved into a musical tradition. The lyrics tend to be sad reflecting the living conditions of the musicians. It started as vocal only songs and changed with the use of makeshift instruments and finally access to proper musical instruments. Blues came to influence most of American popular music of the 20th century, notably [[jazz]] and [[rock]].

# blues as a form, the [[12 bars blues]]
Today blues also refers to a musical [[form]]. Most blues are 12 bars long and feature three [[7 chords]] on the [[tonic]], [[sub dominant]] and [[dominant]]. This heavy use of 7 chords and the movement from I 7 to IV 7 is a signature sound of the blues. 

# blues as a language
The heavy use of dominant 7 chords, the ambiguity between [[Major 3rd]] and [[minor 3rd]] as well as the [[blue note]] used in blues melodies have now become key elements of an improvisational language. It is common in jazz to employ these tools to give a 'blues flavour' to a solo.
